Services

One partner across ops, CRM, executive support, and bookkeeping.

I act as a fractional operations and CRM systems partner who also covers executive support and bookkeeping workflows. Instead of hiring three different people, you get one operator who lives in the tools and owns the process.

Core lanes

Everything I do fits into one of these four lanes, often overlapping in a single engagement.

Ops & CRM systems

Workflows and hygiene in Supermove, RingCentral, Salesforce, and Google Workspace. Making sure leads, jobs, and requests move consistently from intake to completion.

Executive & admin support

Inbox and calendar management, documentation, simple SOPs, and coordination with other stakeholders so decisions move forward and don’t stall in email.

QA & reporting

QA programs for support and ops teams, plus Excel or Sheets-based reports that give leadership a clean view of quality, trends, and workloads.

Bookkeeping support

Xero and QuickBooks Online bookkeeping support: coding, reconciliations, invoice and payment tracking, and connecting job and time data to the books.

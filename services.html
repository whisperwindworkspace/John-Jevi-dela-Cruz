Core lanes

We can start in one lane and add more as trust and context build.

Ops & CRM workflows

I configure and maintain CRM processes, tags, and status flows. I standardise how leads and jobs move through tools like Supermove and RingCentral so your team isn’t reinventing the wheel for every customer.

Executive & administrative support

I handle inbox and calendar management, meeting prep, light research, and documentation. I manage contract workflows like DocuSign and keep track of follow-ups in a way senior leaders can trust.

Bookkeeping & finance support

In Xero and QuickBooks Online, I code transactions, reconcile accounts, track invoices and payments, and build simple reports. I connect what’s happening in your time-tracking/scheduling system with what hits the books, and I field invoice questions and straightforward disputes while your accountant focuses on tax and structure.

QA, analytics & documentation

I use my QA background and Excel skills to build practical reports and dashboards. I document workflows and SOPs so new hires and external partners can actually understand how your system works, not just guess.

How to start working together

Start small, prove the value, then decide how deep you want to go.

Step 1 · Intro and system walkthrough

You walk me through your current tools, workflows, and pain points. We define a small, meaningful scope for the first month across ops, CRM, and/or bookkeeping support.

Step 2 · Trial month execution

I run the agreed workflows, send written weekly updates, and refine playbooks as we go. You get clearer visibility and fewer loose ends.

Step 3 · Review and decision

At the end of the month, we look at what improved and what’s still messy. Then we decide whether to extend, change the scope, or keep it as a project-based relationship.

Step 4 · Scale or keep focused

If the trial works, we either deepen the engagement—more bookkeeping, more CRM automation, more ops ownership—or keep a tight, focused scope that fits your budget and bandwidth.

About

The person behind the systems and dashboards.

I have two boys and am the main breadwinner for the family. I've moved from frontline customer support into QA leadership and into running the back-end of operations: CRM, executive support, and finance workflows. I care about building systems that keep a business steady while the people running it can still have a life. That applies to you as a founder and to me as a parent.

Quick snapshot

  • Core roles: ops & CRM systems, executive support, bookkeeping
  • Background: support agent → QA lead → executive assistant & ops
  • Life outside work: two boys, games, anime, manga, and web novels
  • Operating style: calm, curious, and root-cause focused

How I got here

I didn’t start in strategy. I started where the tickets, calls, and angry customers show up first.

Front-line experience

I spent years on the front line handling calls, emails, and chats. That’s where I learned how broken processes, unclear ownership, and missing context show up for real customers—not just on a slide deck.

Quality & performance

I moved into QA leadership, auditing interactions, building reports in Excel, coaching analysts, and presenting performance to management, clients, and C-level stakeholders. It trained me to spot patterns-not just one-off mistakes.

Executive support & ops

Next, I took on the role of executive assistant and operations, owning inboxes, calendars, documentation, CRM workflows, pipelines for jobs, and financial touchpoints like invoicing and payment tracking in Xero and QuickBooks.

Now: the back-end of the business

Today, I focus on the "brain" of the operation-how work enters your system, how it moves, how it's measured, and how it shows up in your books. My goal is to reduce the number of surprises you and your team have to deal with.

How my life shapes how I work with you

The same reasons I need calm, predictable systems at home are why I build them for the businesses I support.

Family first, but not sloppy

As a parent and a breadwinner, I really can't afford chaos. I mean it when it comes to reliability and follow-through, and yet I also understand founders and teams are human. I design realistic workflows, not idealised fantasy processes that nobody can keep up with.

Curious and root-cause driven

I don't like band-aid fixes. If something keeps breaking-missed leads, messy data, invoice disputes-I want to know why. I dig into the process, the tools, and the incentives so we can fix the underlying issue once instead of firefighting it every week.

Ambitious with direction

I've always been intentional about moving from front line work into the roles that shape the system itself. When I work with you, I'm not just trying to get tasks complete; I'm trying to make your operation cleaner and more self-sustaining quarter after quarter.

Human, not robotic

I enjoy games, anime, and long-form storytelling. I value nuance and context; I don't speak like a script. You get clear, straightforward communication-not jargon for its own sake and not fake cheer when the data says we've got a real problem to solve.

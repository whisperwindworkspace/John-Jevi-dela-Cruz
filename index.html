What I actually do

I sit across ops, CRM, executive support, QA, and bookkeeping so you don’t have to juggle four different part-time hires.

Ops & CRM systems

I set up and maintain workflows in tools like Supermove, RingCentral, Salesforce, and Google Workspace. Leads, tags, and job stages move through a predictable pipeline instead of getting stuck in inboxes or lost in someone’s head.

Executive & admin support

I manage inboxes and calendars, keep documents organised in Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, do light research, and run contract workflows like DocuSign. Version control and follow-through are baked in so nothing quietly disappears.

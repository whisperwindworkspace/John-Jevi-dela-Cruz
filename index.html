About Me

Many service-based businesses don’t just struggle with sales—they struggle with what happens after: quotes, jobs, invoices, payroll, and numbers scattered across inboxes and tools.

I help owners and teams fix that by building calm, reliable systems around operations, bookkeeping, and automation. I’m an Operations & Finance Ops specialist and Executive Assistant with hands-on experience in move logistics, BPO/QA, and remote admin—and I’m also Xero Certified (L1 & L2), a Xero Payroll Specialist – AU, and a QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor.

In practice, that looks like: cleaner inboxes, faster lead responses, consistent quotes and job records, invoices that match the work done, payroll/comp tracking that reconciles, and dashboards that tell you what’s really happening—so month-end becomes a review, not a fire drill.

Services

Bookkeeping, operations, and automation support for small teams, agencies, and service-based businesses that need someone to own the back-end, not just “help out.”

  • Bookkeeping & Finance Ops — Xero/QuickBooks setup support, AR/invoicing, basic AP, bank feed checks, reconciliations, payroll & compensation tracking.
  • Quote-to-Cash for Service Businesses — structuring estimates/quotes, job records, and invoices so every job can be traced from inquiry to payment.
  • Operations & Executive Assistance — calendar and inbox management, travel logistics, file organization, SOP creation, meeting notes and action tracking.
  • Lead Handling & Light Sales Ops — SMS/email lead response, filters/labels, simple pipelines in tools like Supermove, RingCentral, PhoneBurner.
  • Automation & Reporting — Gmail filters, simple workflows, Excel reporting, and Power BI dashboards (iQor experience) for KPIs and visibility.
  • Custom Support Packages — tailored mixes of bookkeeping, EA, CRM workflows, and reporting based on your tools and current bottlenecks.

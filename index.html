How I plug into your existing setup

Designed to reduce founder and senior accountant cognitive load.

Workflow
  • Structured onboarding sprint (2–3 weeks). Map your chart of accounts, bank feeds, payroll, key reports, and recurring workflows. Build a lightweight runbook so there’s no confusion about what “done” looks like each week.
  • Weekly reconciliation & reporting rhythm. Lock in specific days for reconciliations, payables reviews, and variance checks so your numbers don’t drift and you’re not doing “finance firefighting” every month-end.
  • Documentation-first collaboration. I document changes, questions, and edge cases in writing (not just chat threads), so your accountant and future team members can follow what happened, when, and why.
  • Clean handoffs to senior finance. Trial balances, reconciliations, and supporting schedules are packaged so your accountant can focus on high-value advisory instead of chasing missing context.

How I communicate

Clear, low-noise communication so you always know where things stand.

Operating habits
  • Weekly written updates. One summary covering what was done, what’s pending, and any decisions or inputs needed from you — not scattered messages.
  • Documented decisions. When we change a process, I update the written runbook so we don’t forget why we did it that way three months later.
  • Escalation instead of guessing. If something is unclear or high-risk, I escalate quickly rather than silently making assumptions.
  • Respect for founders’ attention. I batch questions and updates so you’re not pulled into context-switching all week.

FAQ

Straight answers to obvious questions.

What kind of engagement are you looking for?
Ideally a part-time, remote role where I can own the bookkeeping backbone (Xero/QBO, payroll, AP/AR) with a clear reporting line to a founder or senior accountant.
Do you work with accountants or directly with founders?
Both. I’m comfortable being the internal “hands on keyboard” for a founder while collaborating with their external accountant, or working as part of an accounting firm’s delivery team.
How do you prefer to communicate?
Written first: clear weekly updates, concise questions, and documented changes. I’m happy to do calls when needed, but I try not to make meetings the default.

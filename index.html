About me

The real problem usually isn’t “not enough tools.” It’s scattered workflows: leads in inboxes, jobs in a CRM, invoices in accounting software, and reporting in a spreadsheet no one trusts.

I help owners and teams fix that by building calm, reliable systems around operations, bookkeeping, and automation. I’ve worked across customer service, QA, and executive support, then layered in accounting skills and certifications so I can bridge what happens on the floor with what shows up in Xero or QuickBooks.

The result is fewer dropped balls and clearer decisions: sub-30-second lead responses instead of missed opportunities, quotes that tie cleanly to invoices, payroll and compensation tracking that reconciles, and dashboards that surface what matters without you living in spreadsheets.

Services

Engagements are scoped around your tools, volume, and gaps. Typical work combines bookkeeping, operations, and light automation.

  • Bookkeeping & finance ops — monthly books in Xero/QBO, invoice monitoring, AR follow-ups, basic AP, reconciliations, and payroll/compensation tracking.
  • Quote-to-cash for service businesses — structuring estimates, jobs, and invoices so every job can be traced from inquiry to payment.
  • Operations & EA support — inbox and calendar management, travel and vendor coordination, file organization, SOPs, and documentation.
  • Lead handling & light sales ops — SMS/email lead response, Gmail filters/labels, and simple CRM flows in tools like Supermove, RingCentral, PhoneBurner.
  • Analytics & reporting — Excel and Power BI dashboards (iQor experience) for revenue, margins, QA metrics, and operational KPIs.
  • Custom systems & automation — workflows and documentation tailored to your stack, not copied from a generic playbook.

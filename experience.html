How I operate inside a team

How I like to work inside your team.

Structured, not chaotic

I prefer clear routines—daily or weekly cadences and defined workflows—over constant last-minute requests. That makes work more predictable for everyone, including your accountant and external partners.

Documented and searchable

When workflows or rules change, I write it down. That way new team members and advisors can see what changed, when it changed, and why we did it.

Escalation instead of guessing

If something is unclear or high-risk, I escalate quickly with options instead of guessing. I’d rather have one short decision-making conversation than a big clean-up later.

Async-first communication

I default to written updates, checklists, and dashboards. Calls are used when they add value, not as the default way to move work forward.
